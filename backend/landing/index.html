FAQ

Common questions

What kind of parts can MechaGen generate?
MechaGen works best with discrete mechanical parts: brackets, housings, mounts, gears, flanges, covers, and similar industrial components. Complex organic or artistic shapes are outside the current scope.
How accurate is the geometry?
Preview mode uses a deterministic geometry planner — dimensions are accurate to the spec. Solid mode runs CadQuery and produces a proper parametric solid. All output is validated through an 8-check mesh pipeline before export.
Can I use my own engineering drawings?
Yes. Upload PNG or PDF engineering drawings and our vision model will extract dimensions, feature annotations, hole patterns, and constraints automatically — then use them to guide generation.
What's the difference between Preview and Solid generation?
Preview is fast (seconds) — it uses our geometry planner to build a Three.js-compatible mesh for visualization and quick export. Solid generation uses CadQuery (industrial-grade parametric modeling) for proper manufacturable geometry. Solid is slower but production-ready.
Is my data private?
Your prompts, blueprints, and generated parts are not shared or used for training without your consent. Enterprise and Team plans include data isolation. See our privacy policy for full details.
What export formats are supported?
OBJ and GLB are available on all plans. STL export (required for most slicers and CNC workflows) is available on Pro and Team plans. STEP export is on the roadmap.
Do I need CAD experience to use MechaGen?
No. MechaGen is designed so engineers at any level can describe what they need in plain language. That said, more precise prompts and better blueprints produce better results — so professionl context helps.
